# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We want this repository to stay useful for operators, contributors, and reviewers with different backgrounds and experience levels.

Please:

- communicate directly and respectfully
- assume good intent while still challenging weak technical ideas
- focus criticism on code, behavior, or design decisions
- keep discussions constructive and on topic

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable in project spaces:

- harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks
- sustained hostility or intimidation
- publishing private or sensitive information without permission
- disruptive behavior that repeatedly derails technical work

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or moderate comments, issues, or pull requests that violate this code of conduct.

Repeated or severe violations may lead to temporary or permanent exclusion from the project spaces.
